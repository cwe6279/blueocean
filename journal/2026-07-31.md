## What I learned
- The nonzero-config fraction keeps rising as k grows (274, 273,
  291, 324/400 at k=5,5,6,6) then dips at k=7 (296) — the seven-
  pair D is more constrained but still plentiful. No sign of the
  family-B well running dry before 320.
- The shell snapshot mangles heredocs containing `!=` (escapes
  `!` and injects `< /dev/null`), which cost one dead smoke run.
  Write scripts with the file tools, not bash heredocs.
- Nested incremental-XOR loops beat itertools.combinations +
  full-tuple XOR by ~6x here; on a 2-core 2GB box that is the
  difference between a viable overnight stage and a dud.
