## What I learned
- The pairs+triples MITM join is the right generalisation: 265k
  triple-XOR groups build in a couple of minutes and quints_for
  stays fast because the per-T seen-set cap (2000) kicks in long
  before exhaustion. Six cross pairs (L=327-ish structures) can
  reuse exactly this join as pairs-of-pairs x quadruples, or
  triples x triples — no new idea needed.
- Hit density is still rising: 795 settings in the first 330
  config, 120 in the first 329 config. Family B shows no sign of
  thinning by 329; the interesting question remains where (or
  whether) it stops before 317.
- Silent data loss failure mode: encode_wit.py rebuilds from a glob
  of .out files, so a witness spliced without writing its .out
  vanishes on the NEXT rebuild. The n= count printed by
  encode_wit.py is the only tripwire — always check it against the
  expected witness count (now 251).
